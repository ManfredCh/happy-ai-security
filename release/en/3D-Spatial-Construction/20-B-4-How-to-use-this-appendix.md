## B.4 — How to use this appendix

Registers material found between the 2026-09-26 search and a search on 2026-10-25. The L0–L5
capability ladder and the three verification contracts (appearance, geometry, physical
serviceability) are unchanged. The L5 rung gained its first product-grade claimant and a safety map
of its own; the environments underneath stayed the month's most-attacked layer. Cite the body
cutoff, Appendix A's date and this appendix's date together.
